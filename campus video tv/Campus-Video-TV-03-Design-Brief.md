Table of Contents
=================

1\. Design Principles
=====================

*   Widescreen, not square — unlike Podcasts’ cover art, video thumbnails follow standard 16:9 framing so they read correctly wherever they end up embedded or shared
*   Playback quality is honest, not hidden — if the player is serving a lower-bitrate stream to save the viewer’s data, that’s a reasonable trade-off, not something to disguise
*   "Voice of UPSA TV" gets flagship visual weight — featured placement on the video home, distinct from other shows, without a separate design system
*   No AI-generic tells — same anti-AI-generic rules as the rest of the portfolio (Campus Mart Design Brief §5)

2\. Visual Direction
====================

No new palette or typography — consistent with every feature since Campus Mart.

*   Campus Teal (#1F7A6C) — play button, progress bar fill, "watching now" indicator
*   Ink Navy (#1B2A4A) — titles, video metadata, the flagship "Voice of UPSA TV" badge
*   Thumbnails — 16:9, auto-generated by Cloudinary from the video itself unless Admin overrides with a custom image

3\. Components
==============

| **Component** | **Purpose** | **Key states** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show Card | Grid item on the video home | Default, flagship (Voice of UPSA TV, visually distinct), featured |
| Video Card | List/grid item within a show page | Default, with duration overlay on the thumbnail |
| Video Player | Embedded on the video page | Playing, paused, buffering, quality indicator (subtle, not intrusive) |
| Report Sheet | Reason picker on a video | "I appear in this and didn’t consent" visually equal-weighted with other reasons, consistent with Campus Gallery’s treatment of the same concern |
| Caption Toggle | On/off control for the caption track, when present | Only rendered when a captions file exists — no empty/disabled control shown otherwise |

4\. Key Screens (Phase 1)
=========================

| **Screen** | **Priority** | **Notes** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Video home (/video) | P0 | "Voice of UPSA TV" featured first, then other shows by category |
| Show page (/video/\[show-slug\]) | P0 | Thumbnail grid of that show’s videos |
| Video page (/video/\[show-slug\]/\[video-slug\]) | P0 | Player, description, captions if present, report action |
| Admin — show list & editor | P0 | Same shape as Podcasts’ show editor |
| Admin — video upload | P0 | Upload with duration/size limits surfaced in the UI before the upload starts, not after it fails |
| Admin — reports | P0 | Consent-based reports shown with the same priority treatment as Campus Gallery’s |
